Privacy & Usage Analytics
This tool counts anonymous page opens and your approximate country (no finer) to gauge usage and prioritize features. It is cookieless and stores no precise location, IP address, mission area, or personal data — nothing from your map is ever sent, and analytics never run offline.

Data proxy URL — canopy + live TFR/NOTAM (optional override — self-host guide ↗)
Leave blank to use the built-in default proxy — the app works out of the box. Enter your own Cloudflare Worker URL to override (e.g. for a fork hosted elsewhere; the built-in proxy only serves the official app origins). The proxy serves CORS-blocked sources: Meta/WRI 1 m canopy tiles (vegetation overlay & viewshed), the FAA TFR GeoServer (live TFRs), the FAA NOTAM Search backend (live NOTAMs), and the USFS/BLM ArcGIS servers (forest roads/trails/MVUM, BLM transport & public-land ownership) — TFRs/NOTAMs auto-fetched per area. NOTAMs come from an unofficial endpoint: treat as advisory and verify officially. Heavy use may briefly rate-limit (⚠ PROXY LIMIT in the header); it clears in about a minute.
